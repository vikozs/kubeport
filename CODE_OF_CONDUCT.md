# Code of Conduct

Kubeport follows the [CNCF Community Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainers at conduct@kosir.info. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
